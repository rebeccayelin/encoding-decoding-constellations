About

Our choice of representation shapes what we can create.

By translating between languages—from the constellation patterns of Islamic art into mathematical graphs and back into geometric patterns—we gain new vocabularies for reasoning and making. Through mixed-media works including laser engravings, wood and fabric sculptures, and animated light pieces, this exhibition explores how mathematical abstraction, computation, and craft intersect to recreate classical designs and open new expressive forms.

At its core is a method by Lin and Kaplan that “encodes” constellation patterns as graphs and “decodes” them into interlocking stars using circle packing and classical constructions.
